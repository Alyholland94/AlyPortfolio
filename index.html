My practice blends branding and creative coding to produce emotionally driven, inclusive design that explores neurodivergent experiences and mental health.

I explore this through hybrid methods that merge design, code and experimentation to create responsive and inclusive visual outcomes.

By combining design and code, I aim to make visible experiences that are often overlooked, encouraging awareness, accessibility and connection through visual communication.
